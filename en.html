Srpska verzija

Summary

Glasalica collects no data about you.

The app requests no permissions on your device, has no user accounts, contains no advertising and uses no analytics.

Data we collect

None.

The app does not collect, store on any server, or share with third parties any of the following:

  • your email address or any other personal information;
  • your location;
  • your contacts, photos, messages or files;
  • device identifiers or advertising identifiers;
  • usage data, analytics or crash reports.

Permissions

The app requests no access to your location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera.

Sensors

On the start screen the game reads the device accelerometer (motion sensor) to detect that you have shaken the phone to start playing. These readings are used only at that moment, in memory, and are never recorded or transmitted.

Data stored on your device

If the game saves your progress or settings, it writes them only to the app's private storage on your device. Neither we nor anyone else can access that data, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Leaderboard

If you want your score to appear on the leaderboard, you type in the name it should be shown under. It does not have to be your real name — any label will do. Only what you type and the score you achieved are sent. Nothing else is sent, and none of it is linked to you or to your device. If you do not enter a name, nothing is sent.

Children

The app is educational and contains no content unsuitable for children. As it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Third-party services

The app uses no third-party SDK, service or library that collects data. It is built with the Godot Engine, which does not itself collect user data.

Changes to this policy

If any of the above changes in a future version of the app, this policy will be updated before that version is published, and the date at the top revised.
