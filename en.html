Privacy Policy — Glasalica

Last updated: 28 September 2026
Application: Glasalica (org.itblokada.glasalica)
Contact: blokadna.igra@gmail.com

Srpska verzija

Summary

Glasalica collects data only if you choose to join the leaderboard.

The app requests no special permissions on your device (other than internet access), has no user accounts, contains no advertising and uses no analytics.

Data we collect

Only if you join the leaderboard: the name you type in, your in-game score and a random identifier the game creates on first launch (not a device identifier or advertising identifier). This data is not shared with third parties.

The app does not collect any of the following:

  • your email address or any other personal information;
  • your location;
  • your contacts, photos, messages or files;
  • hardware device identifiers or advertising identifiers;
  • usage data, analytics or crash reports.

Permissions

The app requests no access to your location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera.

Sensors

On the start screen the game reads the device accelerometer (motion sensor) to detect that you have shaken the phone to start playing. These readings are used only at that moment, in memory, and are never recorded or transmitted.

Data stored on your device

If the game saves your progress or settings, it writes them only to the app's private storage on your device. Neither we nor anyone else can access that data, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Leaderboard

If you want your score to appear on the leaderboard, you type in the name it should be shown under. It does not have to be your real name — any label will do. What you type, the score you achieved and a random install identifier, under which your score is stored, are sent. If you do not enter a name, nothing is sent.

Children

The app is educational and contains no content unsuitable for children. The game asks children for no personal information; joining the leaderboard is optional and the name can be any label.

Third-party services

Leaderboard data is stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database (region: European Union, europe-west1). New game dialogues are downloaded from GitHub without sending any data about you. The app uses no advertising or analytics SDKs. It is built with the Godot Engine, which does not itself collect user data.

Changes to this policy

If any of the above changes in a future version of the app, this policy will be updated before that version is published, and the date at the top revised.

Brisanje podataka / Data deletion — Glasalica

Pošaljite e-mail na blokadna.igra@gmail.com sa naslovom „Brisanje podataka – Glasalica“ i navedite ime pod kojim se pojavljujete na rang-listi. U roku od 30 dana brišemo to ime, vaš rezultat i nasumični identifikator instalacije. Ništa se ne zadržava.

Send an e-mail to blokadna.igra@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion – Glasalica" and state your leaderboard name. Within 30 days we delete that name, your score and the random install ID. Nothing is retained.
